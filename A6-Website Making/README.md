## 📌 About This Activity

> I used **Replit AI** to turn my coffee shop business idea, **"Little Cuppa"**, into a working one-page website. It introduces the brand, lists the menu, and shows the location and contact details.

## 💡 What Have I Learned?

| 🔑 Key Skills | 🧗 Challenge & Solution | 🚀 Future Use |
|---|---|---|
| Building a website using only a written prompt, with no coding. | The result depends on how specific the prompt is, so I included the address, menu items with prices, and contact number. | I can make websites for portfolios, advocacy projects, and health education. |

- ✅ Website creation is now **accessible even to non-IT students**.
- ✅ Clear, detailed prompts produce results closer to what I imagined.
- ✅ A website gives customers 24/7 access to offerings, prices, and contact details.
