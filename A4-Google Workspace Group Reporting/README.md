## 📌 About This Activity

> A **group report** (BSN 4-F, Group 2) that introduced **Google Slides** to the class: what it is, what it is used for, its features, creative uses, and a 20-item quiz to check understanding.

📄 Slides (PDF): [`Reporting-GoogleSlides.pdf`](Reporting-GoogleSlides.pdf)

### 🗂️ Report Outline

| Part | Content |
|:---|:---|
| 🙋 **Meet the Group** | Introduction of the members |
| ❓ **What is Google Slides?** | A web-based presentation app by Google, part of Google Workspace |
| 🎯 **Purpose** | School reports, business presentations, seminars, project proposals, and more |
| ⚙️ **Features** | Real-time collaboration, easy sharing, templates, multimedia, cloud storage, offline access, presenter tools |
| 🎨 **Practical Uses** | Interactive learning modules, digital posters and infographics, online quizzes and games, project planning, digital portfolios |
| 📝 **Quiz** | 15 multiple-choice and 5 true-or-false items |

---

## 💡 What Have I Learned?

| 🔑 Key Skills | 🧗 Challenge & Solution | 🚀 Future Use |
|---|---|---|
| Presenting a tool clearly, collaborating online in one shared file, and writing quiz questions that test understanding. | Coordinating many members on one presentation was difficult, so we used real-time collaboration and comments to edit together. | I can use Google Slides for case presentations, health teaching, and a digital portfolio of my certificates and achievements. |

- ✅ Google Slides is more than a presentation tool. It can also make **posters, quizzes, and portfolios**.
- ✅ **Real-time collaboration** makes group work faster and more organized.
- ✅ A short quiz at the end helps the audience remember the key points.
