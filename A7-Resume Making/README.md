## 📌 About This Activity

> I gave **Claude** and **Gemini** the same personal details and asked each for a **1-page professional resume** as a Registered Nurse. I compared the outputs and then made a **final version** that combines the best parts.

### ⚖️ Comparison

| Criteria | 🟠 Claude | 🔵 Gemini |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Structure** | Conventional, but spilled onto a second page. | Logical order, all on one page. |
| **Formatting** | Simple, with excessive spacing. | Clean, consistent, with subtle blue accents. |
| **Quality** | Complete but less concise. | Concise, with rotations grouped by hospital. |

🏆 **My pick:** Gemini, for its cleaner layout and better use of space.

## 💡 What Have I Learned?

| 🔑 Key Skills | 🧗 Challenge & Solution | 🚀 Future Use |
|---|---|---|
| Using AI to draft a resume and judging output by structure, formatting, and quality. | Claude's version ran past one page, so I compared it with Gemini and built a final one-page version. | I can tailor my resume and cover letters to each job and use AI for interview practice. |

- ✅ Giving **the same prompt to different AI tools** shows their strengths and weaknesses.
- ✅ Grouping clinical rotations by hospital makes a resume easier to scan.
- ✅ AI output should be **verified**, and **patient confidentiality** must always be protected.
